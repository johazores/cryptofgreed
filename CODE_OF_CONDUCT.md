# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Crypt of Greed welcomes constructive participation from people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Expected behavior includes:

- communicating respectfully and clearly;
- discussing ideas and code rather than attacking people;
- giving actionable review feedback;
- accepting correction without retaliation;
- respecting privacy and avoiding disclosure of personal information;
- helping keep issues and pull requests focused and useful.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized language;
- personal insults or sustained hostile behavior;
- publishing private information without permission;
- deliberately disrupting project discussions;
- submitting malicious code, concealed tracking, credential theft, or security exploits;
- pressuring maintainers or contributors through repeated off-topic contact.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces where a participant represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and focused on maintaining a safe, productive project environment.
